# Findings

Finding 1: 6,534 of 96,470 delivered orders (6.77%) arrived after the estimated delivery date.

Finding 2: On-time orders average 4.29 stars. Late orders average 2.27 stars, about 2 stars lower.

Finding 3: 62.4% of late orders got a 1 or 2 star review, against 9.3% of on-time orders. Late orders are about 7% of reviewed orders but about 32% of all unhappy reviews. The other 68% of unhappy reviews come from on-time orders.

Finding 4: The longer the delay, the worse the review. Unhappy reviews (1 or 2 stars): 9.3% on time, 32.1% for 1-3 days late, 67.6% for 4-7 days late, 79.3% for 8+ days late. Even a 1-3 day delay lowers the average review from 4.29 to 3.29.
